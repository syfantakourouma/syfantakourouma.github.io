GET TO KNOW ME

About Me

My journey into data science, analytics and using technology to make a meaningful difference.

My Background

I'm currently studying for a part-time MSc in Data Science and Analytics, having previously completed a Bachelor's degree in Business Information Systems, with a First Class Honours. Through my studies, I'm developing my knowledge and practical skills in data analysis, statistical methods, database management and data visualisation.

I've gained academic experience using tools such as R, SQL,Python, and Microsoft Excel to explore datasets, identify patterns and interpret findings. I'm also developing my skills in Power BI and learning how data can be communicated in a clear and meaningful way.

My Interest

One of my biggest interests is exploring how data science and artificial intelligence can help address real-world challenges.

This is reflected in my proposed dissertation, which explores the feasibility of AI-powered predictive diagnosis for infectious diseases in Guinea.With a personal connection to Guinea through my family, I'm particularly interested in how data-driven approaches could contribute to healthcare research and potentially support underserved communities.

My Career Goals

My long-term goal is to build a career in data science and analytics, using technology and analytical thinking to turn data into meaningful insights and create a positive impact.
